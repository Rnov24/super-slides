# Reference 02: Visual Taste Discovery & Token Definition Playbook

This playbook defines visual taste standards, token architecture, and typographic hierarchy for `super-slides`.

Decks generated by AI agents must look custom-crafted, intentional, and authoritative. Default-looking AI slop, flat untextured canvases, generic system-ui typography, and floating top-heavy layouts are banned.

---

## 1. The Core Visual Mandates

### Mandate A: Atmosphere Over Flat Surfaces (Flat Backgrounds Banned)
Flat, solid-color backgrounds without depth or atmosphere are strictly banned.
Every presentation canvas must establish an environmental surface:
- **Dark decks**: Use multi-stop radial gradients, dark ambient glows, subtle geometric grids (e.g., 40px hairline grids at 25% border opacity), or dot-matrix textures.
- **Light decks**: Use warm tactile paper tones (such as `#f8f6f1` cream), subtle radial lighting washes, and hairline rules. Pure flat `#ffffff` with no texture or framing is prohibited.

### Mandate B: Projector Typography & The Weight Ladder
Generic system-ui display titles are banned. Slide typography must obey two strict legibility rules:

1. **The 18px Body Floor (R-16)**:
   - Body text never goes below 18px on any slide.
   - Never shrink body text to 10px - 14px to fix crowding; cut copy or change layout instead.
2. **The Weight Ladder**:
   - Bigger type is thinner, smaller type is bolder:
     - Giant display headlines (>= 80px): weight 400.
     - Medium headings (28px - 48px): weight 700.
     - Body text (18px - 22px): weight 400 or 500 (never 300 thin).
3. **Didone Display Faces Banned**:
   - High-contrast Didone display faces (Bodoni, Playfair, Didot) with extreme thick-to-thin hairlines are strictly BANNED for slide headers. Hairlines vanish under 1280px conference projectors. Permitted display faces have consistent, sturdy stroke weight: `Archivo Black`, `Sora 700`, `Space Grotesk`.

### Mandate C: Split-Contrast Tokens
Every theme must publish split-contrast accent tokens to guarantee WCAG 4.5:1 legibility across all backgrounds:
- `--accent`: Core visual accent for lines, dots, and borders.
- `--accent-ink`: Darkened or specialized tone for small text sitting on light surfaces, ensuring >= 4.5:1 contrast.
- `--accent-on`: High-contrast text color for labels sitting directly on a solid accent fill (typically `#ffffff` or deep black).

### Mandate D: Visual Rhythm & Pacing Director
A monotonous presentation where every slide has identical visual density leads to audience fatigue. The generator orchestrates an alternating visual rhythm:

1. **Pole / Release Slides**:
   - Quiet, high-impact slides featuring a single bold thesis (`layouts/pole-hero.html`) or giant open numbers 220px+ (`layouts/pole-stat.html`).
   - Spatial Anchoring for R-11: Pole slides satisfy R-11 (vertical span >= 65%) by placing the top header at y=72px and bottom context/proof chips at y=800-880px, leaving the center open and uncluttered.
2. **Analytical Slides**:
   - Dense, structured slides presenting diagrams, matrices, comparison tables, or algorithmic traces (`layouts/split-canvas.html`, `layouts/open-columns.html`, `layouts/full-diagram.html`).
3. **Pacing Cadence**:
   - Avoid placing more than two analytical slides in succession without a pole or release slide to reset cognitive load.

### Mandate E: Layout Variety (Zero "Box Prison")
Enclosing content in heavy card containers (`.surface` card boxes) creates a cluttered, amateur appearance.
Rules:
- Eliminate container cards: let content breathe directly on the canvas.
- Separate columns and sections using 1px hairline dividers only (`border-left: 1px solid var(--surface-border)`).
- No two consecutive slides may share the same structural layout.

### Mandate F: Strict Copy Budget (<= 40 Body Words Per Slide, R-15)
- Body prose per slide must not exceed 40 words.
- Action titles, kickers, data table cells, and SVG diagram labels are excluded from this 40-word limit.
- Cut filler copy ruthlessly rather than shrinking font sizes below 18px.

## 2. The Four Theme Presets

### Preset 1: Bold Signal (High-Velocity Dark Engineering)
- **Mood**: Confident, modern, high-impact technical architecture.
- **Fonts**: `archivo-black.css`, `space-grotesk.css`
- **Tokens**:
  - `--bg`: `#0a0d12` (dark radial gradient)
  - `--surface`: `#121822`
  - `--surface-border`: `#222d3d`
  - `--text`: `#f0f6fc`
  - `--text-muted`: `#8b949e`
  - `--accent`: `#FF5722` (focal orange)
  - `--accent-ink`: `#ff7043`
  - `--accent-on`: `#ffffff`
  - `--accent-alt`: `#38bdf8` (electric cyan)

- **Fonts**: `sora.css`, `dm-sans.css`
- **Tokens**:

  - `--surface`: `#ffffff`
  - `--surface-border`: `#e6e2d9`

  - `--text-muted`: `#5a554e`
  - `--accent`: `#b33927` (terracotta)
  - `--accent-ink`: `#8e2819`

### Preset 3: Ink Paper (Classical Parchment & Deep Ink)

## 3. WCAG 4.5:1 Contrast Floor

Every text element must maintain a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 against its underlying surface:
- Never use low-contrast gray text on dark backgrounds.
- Never place muted accent text directly on bright colored badges.
- Ensure dark surfaces retain sufficient contrast under washed-out conference projector conditions.
